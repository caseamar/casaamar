feat(platform): automatically refresh when a new platform version is deployed

- Checks platform-manifest.json every 30 seconds while a page is open.
- Checks immediately when the tab becomes visible, window gains focus, or page is restored.
- Shows a brief Danish update notice before automatic reload.
- Reloads with a cache-busting URL parameter.
- Works across Mission Control, all platform areas and the public website.
- F5 is no longer required to receive a deployed platform update.
- Strengthened no-store/no-cache headers for manifest, shell and HTML.
